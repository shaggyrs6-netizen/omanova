# OMANOVA 0.1.9 — Observatory redesign

The Observatory now uses the same panel layout across all 29 pages. Start on Tonight, follow a pass into Orbit, or go from a star-chart selection to an observing plan.

- **Tonight and Orbit.** The local sky and globe sit above tiles for the next pass, cloud cover and Moon. Scroll down for the hourly night overview. Open Orbit carries the selected station, pass and time into the detailed track. Switch between the globe and flat map or scrub through the pass.
- **Star Chart and My Night.** Inspect an object beside the chart, move the time slider in the observing site's time zone, and add the selection directly to My Night. Choose a saved pick to see its timing chart.
- **Conditions and sky tools.** Inspect the forecast for a selected pass, including cloud layers and hourly coverage where available. Sky and spaceflight catalogues pair selectable lists with details. Missing and stale forecasts remain labelled.
- **Planning and logging.** Compare Home with a destination, select multiple events for a calendar or offline guide, and browse dated log entries alongside the selected observation or draft. “I saw it” and CSV export remain available.
- **Settings and equipment.** Settings groups its controls into six sections. My Equipment and Sky separates equipment preferences, saved setups and observing suggestions. Narrow windows can stack the panels. Desktop theme and red observing mode remain available.
- **Updating older installations.** The in-app updater recognises the known older worker service configuration, including an unset systemd data-directory variable. See the [upgrade instructions](../README.md#upgrading-an-existing-installation) for installations that predate the updater.

The bundled comet catalogue covers four targets from 18 November 2026 through 2 January 2027 at 00:00 UTC. Track samples are two hours apart. The displayed brightness estimates come from dated observations on 9–14 November and can change.
